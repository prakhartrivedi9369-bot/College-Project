01 / PHILOSOPHY

Learning should leave a trace.

Most study systems measure whether something was read.This MINI PROJECT focuses on what happens after reading.

You enter a sprint with incomplete certainty. You make decisions under time pressure. You explain your reasoning to another learner. Then you return to the material with evidence about what you actually understand.

The goal isn't to know everything. It's to know what you can do without being rescued.

02 / THE METHOD

Four steps. One useful habit.

This MINI PROJECT turns ordinary study sessions into small performance experiments. Every sprint follows the same basic loop.
